# Mysterious Hearts Campaign Editorial

## Synopsis

Editorial workspace for existing and planned Mysterious Hearts campaigns. Existing project sources and confirmed user decisions remain authoritative; no campaign text has been imported or revised during setup.

## Tone & Style

Preserve each route's existing narrative viewpoint, tense, and character voice.
There is no imposed project-wide viewpoint, tense, or thematic conclusion.
Read the existing scene and its era-specific references before revising it.

## Notes

This is an editorial starter, not the game's runtime source or a completed
manuscript import. Read [the source map](../SOURCE_MAP.md) and
[the project rules](../../AGENTS.md) first. The
[campaign revision plan](../../roadmap/CAMPAIGN_REVISION_PLAN.md) owns protected
outcomes and the distinction between confirmed facts and proposed staging.

The `serial` form groups ongoing campaign work for this tool; it creates no
publication schedule, word-count target, release order, or gameplay commitment.
No premise or counter-premise is imposed on the nine distinct campaign routes.
Characters, chapters, branches, and knowledge records are not populated yet.
