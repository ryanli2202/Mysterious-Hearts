# Mysterious Hearts portrait art style

Use the selection portraits of Garrett, Rei, Minsu, Emily, April, Sophie, Samantha, and Amy in this directory as the visual references. Use the current unversioned PNGs. Attach the character's existing portrait for likeness and one or two of the eight references for drawing style. Identify each image's role in the prompt.

## Drawing the character

- Draw contemporary romance anime characters with adult proportions. Keep each person's jaw, nose, eye shape, complexion, build, and posture distinct. Let the brows, eyelids, and corners of the mouth carry the expression.
- Use fine, tapered dark contours. Give the outer silhouette and upper lashes a little more weight than the nose, lips, and clothing seams. Minsu's jacket shows how a few folds can describe the weight of an entire sleeve.
- Group hair into readable locks or curls. Follow their direction with narrow highlights and a few fine strands at the silhouette. Preserve the character's hair texture.
- Start with a local color and a clear shadow shape. Look at the shadows below the fringe and jaw in the reference portrait. Use soft transitions sparingly on cheeks and skin. Keep the face readable beside dark hair or clothing.
- Give accessories and identifying marks enough detail to survive the selection-card size. Use Rei and Samantha for tattoo placement and line density, and Emily for fine fabric and ribbon details.

## Framing and delivery

Center one figure, with the full hairstyle and both shoulders visible. Use a relaxed front or slight three-quarter pose and frame down to the hips. Compare the size of the head and the bottom crop with a selection portrait viewed at the same display height. For an outfit variant, match the existing character's face, scale, and eye line.

Export an RGBA PNG with genuine transparency around the silhouette and through open gaps. Keep scenery, cast shadows on the ground, lettering, signatures, and borders out of the image. Use the filename the game loads for that character and outfit.

## Reusable generation prompt

> Draw [character] as a hip-up portrait for Mysterious Hearts. Use image 1 for [face, complexion, build, hair, identifying details]. Use images 2 and 3 for line weight, facial proportions, cel shadows, and framing. Dress the character in [outfit] with [pose and expression]. Use fine tapered outlines, grouped hair highlights, and clear fold shadows under soft light. Center the figure with the hair and shoulders inside the canvas. Deliver a transparent PNG containing only the character.

## Acceptance check

View the result beside the identity reference and a style reference at selection-card size. Check likeness first, then eye line, shadow edges, and silhouette. Inspect the full-size PNG over both a pale and a dark background for halos, clipped curls, stray marks, and filled gaps around hands or glasses. Recheck the face and signature accessories after every revision.
