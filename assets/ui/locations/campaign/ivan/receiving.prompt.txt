Production 16:9 location painting for Mysterious Hearts, clean semi-realistic illustrated manhwa with restrained ink outlines and softly dimensional shading. An ordinary receiving desk and dispatch waiting area inside a modest waterfront commercial building, charcoal blue walls, scuffed dark wooden counter, plain closed delivery folders with no readable writing, a simple clock without legible numerals, two chairs, a glassless solid side exit door visibly unobstructed, warm practical lamp and muted rainy late-afternoon light through high windows. This is the place where an ordinary dispatch worker asks for their shift exit and finishing time to be respected. No people, weapons, gang insignia, logos or readable text. Believable architecture and clear walkable foreground for dialogue portraits, no collage, no glamour or throne-room styling.
