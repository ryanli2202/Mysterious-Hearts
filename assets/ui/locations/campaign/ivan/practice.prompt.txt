Production 16:9 location painting for Mysterious Hearts visual novel, semi-realistic illustrated manhwa with restrained ink contours and soft dimensional shading, muted charcoal slate and cool dusk light. Empty private network practice room in a modest waterfront building, broad clear dark padded floor, simple exposed beams, one long high window with vague rainy harbour lights, padded wall on left, closed plain entrance door at rear right, a bench and folded towels along rear wall. No people, training weapons, logos, readable lettering or grandiose throne-room decoration. Cinematic wide believable architectural perspective, substantial clear foreground for portrait staging and the later group confrontation. Practical contemporary martial training space. One continuous painting, no panels or collage.
