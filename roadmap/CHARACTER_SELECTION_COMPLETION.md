## Access and behaviour

- Main menu → Create your own / Edit selected → **Writing, identity & packages** opens writing assistance, locks, bookmarks, selective randomisation, identity controls, package import/export and illustrative dialogue. All use the existing provider through explicit requests. No provider setup, credentials or account configuration changed.
- Expand, Rewrite and Format keep an editable proposal apart from the original. Applying requires a facts/privacy review checkbox. Exact wording locks, field lengths, explicit age contradictions and format-only word preservation are checked mechanically. Fact locks remain visible review obligations; semantic checking is deliberately not described as infallible. Changes to draft identity/content, restoration or locks invalidate pending results; cancellation discards late responses. An asynchronous request flushes pending recovery edits before recording its revision, avoiding false staleness on the next autosave tick.
- Locks and up to 60 ideas are draft-owned and survive existing revisions/recovery. Applying ideas or randomisation creates a reversible checkpoint. Local randomisation supports MBTI, hairstyle and interests, preserving all locked and unselected fields. It never generates art or reviews a Trait. Unused ideas never enter a reusable profile or dialogue.
- Identity separates legal name, public/preferred address, underworld alias, familiar nickname and pronunciation. Public views omit private identity links. Story-owned explicit disclosures are available in **Tools → Trait applications & identity disclosure · in person**. Direct, group, suggestion and resident dialogue use permitted views; group background only includes disclosures common to the audience. Current employment takes precedence over the original occupation in story dialogue. Selected faction colleagues can recognise a role/alias without learning a private legal-name link. Public biography remains explicitly player-authored public text.
- The neutral sample conversation is non-canonical. It receives public context plus only messages deliberately typed in that sample, never unused ideas, private-history fields or story memory. Draft edits require restarting the sample. Nothing in the sample updates a story, world clock, relationship, quest or memory.
- Portable packages contain `character.json` and a transparent PNG. Export previews the last explicitly saved reusable profile, with private history/identity excluded by default. No provider data, story progress, ideas, revisions or sample conversations are exported. Imports inspect before creating an independent draft; same-name profiles are never overwritten. Version, member names, duplicate/extra files, compressed/expanded size, image format/dimensions and profile/origin schemas are checked. There is no zip extraction. Imported Trait approvals, specialisation and signature acceptance are cleared. Source-format/digest provenance is retained. Existing export files are not overwritten.
- The career/origin picker now includes the complete eleven-criminal-preset catalogue, a custom small-crew founder, narrative backgrounds, previous career/rank, and career-interest saved listings. A returning professional has no active wages or workplace authority until employed. Senior prior experience grants a separately represented craft credential; manager experience adds leadership and an origin portfolio. It creates no completed local shift, paid project, cash, relationship or assignment. Local department budget and further advancement remain earned. Existing founder/recruitment and job/faction progression systems consume the actual seeded records.
- The Combat step distinguishes unawakened, awakened without a named Trait and awakened with a reviewed custom Trait. Latent mana potential is explicit and does not promise an awakening. All seven experiences resolve one shared Path/stat/movebook configuration. An unspecified trained kit receives visible thematic defaults; explicit art/move choices remain bounded. Shinokami arts require active membership or explicit prior training. The move picker includes role/tier filters, learning labels, costs and selected technique details.
- **Trait builder & practice** provides optional structured writing prompts, tier-aware kit/coverage/resource review and real-resolver amplification comparisons. One accepted-aspect specialisation reduces its baseline mana by one (minimum two), increasing other aspects by one (maximum eighteen); ordinary refinement scaling then applies. Changing reviewed mechanics opens an old/new comparison with costs, roles, applications and invalidated signature approval before Apply/Cancel. No unrelated domain is granted.
- Awakened custom-Trait builds at Veteran or above may explicitly request three role/visual-theme-guided signature candidates and accept one or skip. Every candidate is checked against accepted aspect roles, tags, resources and the existing workshop validator. The stored blueprint and input context are revalidated before use; changed mechanics cannot be smuggled through approval flags. Downgrading parks an old signature as a draft idea rather than teaching it. A new story stores the origin signature separately, without consuming the first normal in-story absorption or creating fake learning history.
- Practice opens a separate **In person · disposable creator practice** window, never a phone activity. It executes the actual combat resolver, with technique/amplification/terrain selection against a labelled restrained training target. The sandbox has no real story pointer; encounter completion, rewards, learning, observation/disclosure, cooldowns and world-time helpers are excluded. Editing the creator invalidates that practice snapshot.
- Gameplay **Tools → Trait applications & identity disclosure · in person** offers two explicitly authored uses when accepted aspect tags support them: one ordinary precision-assisted workplace check (+1 craft, ten minutes, four daily energy) and one public-surroundings survey (five minutes, three daily energy, no secret/quest unlock). Both enforce physical presence, activity/commitment restrictions, resources and once-per-day limits. Receipt IDs survive reload, and mutation commits only after the action succeeds. Visible use is recorded for present witnesses without disclosing private history or identity links. Unsupported capabilities/contexts remain unavailable.
- The existing menu, all canonical biographies, Emily's active Critical Hit/Ascension, stable custom roster identity, six-step navigation, explicit save/start, history pins, recovery comparison and read-only saved-progress features remain intact. The checklist now links to the first required field. New tool pages scroll with keyboard focus, and compact picker columns wrap text instead of clipping labels.

## Numerical decisions and limits

`combat_balance.py` owns the scale. Explicit training, recognised narrative experience and active criminal role use the strongest experience floor, never summed whole packages. Current/prior careers use the stronger rank once, with profession-specific power/refinement/conditioning/tactical contributions. Faction branch practice adds a bounded role-specific contribution. Unawakened starts keep power/refinement zero and receive authored physical force/endurance/initiative expressions, never mana or superhuman master techniques. Exceptional awakened conditioning is a small shared-resolver multiplier, not a canonical Trait or arena title.

The seven bands are labelled lower D, upper D, lower C, upper C, lower/mid B, upper B and low A. These are baseline assessments, not promises that every chosen loadout or arbitrary generated Trait wins a matchup.

Actual seeded evidence: [creator-balance-results.json](creator-balance-results.json), produced by `python tools/creator_balance_matrix.py`. Twenty-four comparisons × twelve runs = **288 resolver bouts**. All adjacent packages beat the preceding package in a majority of these representative runs (awakened 8–12/12; unawakened 9–12/12). Exceptional unawakened won 11/12 versus arena rank 10, 10/12 versus rank 2, 5/12 versus enhanced Richter and 0/12 versus Rei. Exceptional awakened without signature won 12/12, 11/12, 3/12 and 0/12 respectively; the representative one-signature build won 12/12, 12/12, 8/12 and 1/12. This is a finite contact-Trait/four-art sample with healthy entry and temporary advanced forms disabled, not exhaustive proof about all Traits, careers, environments or player strategies.

## Persistence and compatibility

Restart the game to load changed Python code. Existing stories retain their saved combat profiles, jobs, affiliations, Path progress, learned skills, funds and history; origins are not reapplied. Fresh story creation now fixes the Path entry clock immediately and stores the optional signature ledger at that point. Future stories from reusable profiles resolve the current documented starting balance. Editing a reusable profile never edits its existing stories.

Draft additions use optional defaults. Imported characters are separate drafts and require explicit final save plus fresh Trait review. New profile fields include visibility, optional Trait/latent-mana choices, specialisation, reviewed origin signature and import provenance. All recovery, undo and mutation errors keep manual editing available. Provider semantic quality, arbitrary future powers and exhaustive simultaneous multi-process writes remain outside the evidence claimed here.
