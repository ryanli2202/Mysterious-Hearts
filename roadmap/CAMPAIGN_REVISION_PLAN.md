## 4. Shared editorial method

### 4.1 Let consequences carry the argument

For each scene mark: protagonist's immediate want; obstacle; supporting character's independent want; observed change; information gained; unresolved feeling; and why the next scene follows. If a paragraph only restates the scene's moral meaning, remove it, compress it or turn it into a later observable consequence.

Retain necessary evidence, consent, outcome and knowledge distinctions at the point where misunderstanding would matter. Do not mechanically delete every negative construction. Move developer-facing restrictions to authoring metadata, UI help or briefing text when they do not belong in a viewpoint's experience. A player still needs clear fixed-history/fixed-outcome framing before committing to an encounter.

Preserve concrete motifs and their changing meaning: Rei's chosen marks, Garrett's shared meals, Martos's kept commitments, Elias's actual artistic work, Camila's decisions under uncertainty. Do not attach a traumatic origin to every hobby or conclude every ordinary visit with a lesson.

### 4.2 Differentiate voices without replacing personalities

| --- | --- | --- |

Keep flirtatious/social energy where appropriate without forcing romance. |
| Charles | Practical reserve, protective fear, responsibility and love | Give him incomplete answers and actions he must sustain afterward. An admission does not immediately cure his habit of giving instructions. No polished speech that makes his conduct reasonable. |
| Rei | Visual attention, quiet humour, thoughtfulness, anger and uncertainty | Let objects and unfinished responses carry feeling. He may give an inconvenient or uncertain answer instead of perfectly naming his own process. |

Do not make each a substitute counsellor or have all four deliver the same reassurance. |

## 5. Existing campaign revision packages

### CR-G — Garrett: keep the victories; change what they are allowed to mean

**Targets:** GD01–GD02, GD05–GD18, GD21–GD25, GD28–GD39 and GD40–GD42; `charles_quest.py`, `garrett_chapters.py`, historical sheets and derivative lore.

1. **Clarify the historical failure.** Keep the school assault and Charles's apology. Preserve Garrett's anger and choice to remain with him. Remove any derivative narration that treats the beating as the source of legitimate authority or necessary moral education.
2. **Make the present recurrence explicit without a lecture.** In GD22 Charles recognises fear and still falls back on force. Garrett recognises the old pattern and still challenges him. Neither narrator nor objective text describes winning as earning adulthood, a right to family or entitlement to be heard. The actual mandatory fight and winner remain unchanged.
3. **Separate defeat from accountability.** After the real victory, preserve existing disclosures and phone-number timing. Add a short proposed exchange in GD23 in which Charles owns making the fight a condition: Garrett's right to an answer existed before it. Possible anchor: “If I'd lost, would you still think I didn't deserve an answer?” / “You deserved one before you came.” His later acknowledgement must be his decision, not magical persuasion by damage.
4. **Make repair observable.** In GD24 and GD37–GD38, Charles keeps the existing contact agreement, asks before directing Garrett and accepts the selected reconciliation boundary. Allow a small lapse into instruction followed by an actual correction. Preserve guarded reconciliation without making it secretly worse than immediate warmth.
5. **Retain the limit of this solution.** Progress still requires a victory. Prose can criticise that demand, but cannot make the encounter mechanically optional. Record this residual tension honestly in editorial review; do not claim it is fully removed unless a future user decision authorises a different gate.
6. **Vary the search's emotional work.** Give the document stages distinct dramatic functions: hope, disappointment, an awkward request to Mei, a boundary at the public desk, temptation to accuse, and recognition through family memory. Keep findings, incorrect-answer delays and evidence order; reduce repeated paraphrases of the same uncertainty warning.
7. **Put Garrett's charm and pride under pressure.** In existing Red Dragons scenes, let him initially enjoy a grateful audience or want the memorable answer. A resident or colleague identifies a practical flaw. He changes a concrete arrangement rather than receiving automatic universal approval.
8. **Make reform credible without inventing a new economy.** Use established crew/time/resource limits to make the chosen complaint or escort arrangement specific. Show resistance, an inconvenient complaint or a declined escort inside existing scenes. Honour established charter acceptance and mission outcomes; do not add a rogue-crew war, protection fee or new revenue system. Identify any necessary extra persistent consequence as separate implementation work.

**Acceptance:** a reader can explain why Charles fought, why he was wrong, what Garrett actually won, and what Charles must change afterward. Both reconciliation endings remain valid; the search stays private; real combat and clue gates still operate. The civilian policies feel tested without undoing their adoption. Ordinary meals retain humour and purpose beyond recovery.
