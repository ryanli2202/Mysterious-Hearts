### Martos: detailed route treatment

[What He Holds](MARTOS_CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md) expands the original eight-beat sketch into fourteen proposed chapters and eight integrated memories:

1. Conventional military service and special-forces selection establish an earned belief in discipline, mutual reliance and protection.
2. Escalating heinous assignments expose his conscious complicity. A final disposal/abandonment order leads to deliberate refusal, limited rescue and desertion.
3. A precarious fugitive interval makes a dependable patron attractive. The Crow recruits him, keeps costly promises and supplies resources for sustained Rage-control work.
4. Martos comes to treat reliability and explicit ownership of decisions as grounds for absolute obedience. He knowingly accepts harmful Syndicate work; the Crow's candour does not make that service morally sound.
5. Present-day protection of a survivor who remembers his participation forces honest acknowledgement. His former commander now threatens the survivor and her agreed evidence transfer.
6. The climax tests protection, Rage control and reliance on the other Aces. The survivor retains agency; completing the mission does not earn forgiveness or automatically redeem Martos. He remains loyal to the Crow.

The detailed plan includes provisional ages/cast, recruitment stages, worldview, relationship payoffs, dialogue anchors, choices, endings and production checks. Its specific history and characters remain proposed.

### Elias: detailed route treatment

2. He personally defeats every incoming Syndicate force deployed against the family. Those victories and the Syndicate's losses are real; no rigged audition or fraudulent talent.
3. The Crow personally defeats a capable Elias at **Elias's family crime headquarters**, producing his first inferiority, fear and awe. The user-confirmed location replaces the earlier Syndicate-HQ proposal.
4. The organisation becomes an affiliate. Elias enters direct service and later becomes Hearts without taking the family leadership he rejected.

6. A proposed present family faction wants its former champion to restore independence. Elias resolves the challenge for the Crow, learning more disciplined service while his fixation persists.

### Camila: detailed route treatment

[The Inheritance](CAMILA_CAMPAIGN_PLAN.md) replaces the generic financial-investigation sketch with fourteen proposed chapters and eight memories:

1. The Crow adopts her from an orphanage and personally raises her. Childhood care, interests and routines establish a family relationship alongside his long-term design.
2. His lessons shape her understanding of resources, institutions and power. She develops her own extraordinary competence, personal life and earned role as Diamonds.
3. He explicitly prepares her as his genuine successor. She already knows this at the present opening; memories deepen its meaning rather than inventing amnesia or a false-heir twist.
4. A real institutional crisis tests more than her financial skill. The Crow gives her a bounded mandate without secretly engineering every loss or handing her current command of the organisation.
5. She combines evidence and colleagues' expertise, negotiates cooperation and chooses among viable plans with different costs. Analysis does not guarantee a perfect answer.
6. The climax resolves that crisis through a decision she can defend and own. She remains daughter, protégée, Diamonds and intended successor; the Crow remains Cardmaster.

Her proposed emotional foundation combines familial attachment, trust, identity and ambition. The treatment separately records authority, disclosure, chronology, relationship payoffs, choices, ending conditions and runtime reconciliation work.
