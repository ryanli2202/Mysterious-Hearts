## Reading and progression contract

- The main route carries motivation, evidence, major decisions and consequences. Social beats include a short main-route acknowledgement where important, with an option to linger for fuller conversation or choose privacy. Not every supporter needs a separate trip or a compulsory conversation.
- Use shared venues to connect scenes, with clear elapsed-time transitions rather than stacking several supporters into one implausible evening. Insert ordinary activity between heavy disclosures. Companion scenes do not replace private reflection.
- Each memory has a present-day trigger, historical scene and explicit return that changes the present conversation. Do not chain several flashbacks without an intervening present-day action. Use the active protagonist's close third-person viewpoint.
- Existing memory gates currently impose broad completion requirements. The chapter placements below require a planned gate refactor: gate each necessary memory at its relevant beat, preserve old completion IDs, and honour already-running dialogue. This document alone does not change those gates.
- Preserve authoritatively resolved evidence, access, mission victories, relationship state and disclosures. Scenes cannot invent rewards, travel, success or completed actions. Friends support solo missions before departure or after return, never inside restricted encounters.
- Every scene and memory runs in the in-person window. Any phone recap remains dark and summarises only experienced/disclosed events. Historical art and actors never relocate present-day NPCs.
